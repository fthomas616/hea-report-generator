# 🏡 Home Energy Analysis (HEA) Report Generator
**Built by an EG4 user, for the EG4 community**

A free, privacy-first tool that helps you understand your solar system's performance, validate your savings, and optimize your time-of-use (TOU) strategy.

---

## 📊 What This Tool Does

- Scans your utility bill and extracts data automatically (PEPCO currently supported)
- Imports your EG4 inverter logs (handles multi-tab XLS files)
- Calculates TOU vs Flat rate savings - see exactly how much you're saving
- Tracks battery efficiency - understand your system's real-world performance
- Monitors peak coverage - how much of your peak usage is covered by solar/battery
- Month-over-month tracking - watch your cumulative savings grow
- Generates professional PDF reports - perfect for sharing or keeping records

---

## 🔐 Privacy First

- ✅ All calculations happen locally on your device
- ✅ Your utility bills never leave your phone
- ✅ No data sent to servers
- ✅ No account required, no tracking

This is a Progressive Web App (PWA) - it works in your browser and can be installed to your home screen like a native app.
